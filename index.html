Mozambique

Mozambique is located in southeastern Africa and is known for its beautiful Indian Ocean beaches. Mozambique boasts coastal cities like Maputo and Maxixe, with their markets and traditional cuisine. Additionally, it features rivers, forests, and wildlife reserves. Its culture is very rich, and visiting Mozambique offers a glimpse into African life and traditions.
