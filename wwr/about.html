White Rapids Rafting

White Rapids Rafting combines fun and adventure into a unique experience that will leave you wanting to come back again and again. Our goal is to ensure everyone has a safe, enjoyable, affordable, and environmentally responsible rafting trip that connects them with nature and creates lasting memories with their friends.

History

In 2010, White Rapids Rafting was founded as a small family-run rafting operation. Since our initial start, we have worked hard to expand the nature of our adventure offerings to include some of the best white water rapids in the country, thus providing an unlimited number of happy customers and their families with cherished memories that will last a lifetime!
